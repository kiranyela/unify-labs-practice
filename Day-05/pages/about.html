About DevConf 2026

Empowering developers to build the future.

Our Mission & History

At DevConf, our mission is to empower developers to build a better future. We believe in the power of code to solve complex problems and improve lives.

Founded in 2015, DevConf has grown from a small local meetup to a major international event. Over the past decade, we have hosted thousands of attendees and hundreds of speakers from around the globe.

Why Attend?

  • Stay ahead of the curve with cutting-edge technology talks.
  • Connect with a diverse community of passionate developers.
  • Get inspired by keynotes from industry visionaries.

Who Should Attend?

  1. Frontend, Backend, and Full-Stack Developers
  2. DevOps Engineers and SREs
  3. Data Scientists and AI Researchers
  4. Product Managers and Tech Leads
