Registration Questions

When does registration close?
Early bird registration closes on Jan 31st, 2026. Standard registration is open until March 1st.
Can I get a refund if I can't attend?
Yes, full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. 50% refund up to 7 days before.
Do you offer group discounts?
Yes, for groups of 5 or more, please contact us for a 15% discount code.

Event Details

Where is the venue located?
The conference is held at the Hitech City Convention Centre, Hyderabad.
Is lunch provided?
Yes, a buffet lunch and snacks are provided on all three days.
Will sessions be recorded?
All main stage sessions will be recorded and provided to attendees within 2 weeks.

Accommodation

Are there partner hotels?
We have partnered with the Novotel and Westin for discounted rates.
Is there a shuttle service?
Shuttles will run from partner hotels to the venue every 30 minutes.

Technical Requirements

Do I need to bring a laptop?
For workshops, a laptop is required. For general sessions, it's optional.
Is Wi-Fi available?
Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the venue.

Networking

Is there an after-party?
Yes, on Day 2 evening at the rooftop lounge. Entry is included with your ticket.
How can I connect with speakers?
There are dedicated "Ask Me Anything" zones after each session.
